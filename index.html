Meet Tucker!

This happy boy is on site every day to make sure you get the experience you deserve.

Thank you for choosing 10/6 Barber Company!


In 2005, I began my career in the hairstyling industry as a co-op student at a local hair salon. This led to a permanent position as a receptionist while I attended St. Lawrence College in the cosmetology program, earning my diploma in 2010.


After graduating, I was offered an incredible opportunity at a local barbershop, where I began training and eventually specialized in barbering. Since then, I've had the opportunity to work with and learn from some pretty amazing barbers. Seeing how different shops operate helped shape the vision for the shop I've built for myself and my community.


10/6 Barber Company is a place to relax and slow down. Whether you're planning a family trip to the barbershop, a group package with friends, or a personal and relaxing getaway on your own, I want you to make the experience your own.


I've put together a service menu with something for all ages, and I look forward to seeing you for your custom service soon!
